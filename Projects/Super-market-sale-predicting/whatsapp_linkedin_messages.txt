TIPS FOR MAXIMUM ENGAGEMENT:
============================

1. Add emojis strategically (2-3 max) to make it visually appealing
2. Include specific numbers (99.92% is impressive!)
3. Create curiosity with a question or bold statement
4. Keep it concise (WhatsApp users prefer short messages)
5. Include relevant hashtags (but not too many - 2-3 is ideal)
6. Add a clear call-to-action
7. Post at optimal times (evenings/weekends get more engagement)
8. Personalize the message based on your audience

BEST PRACTICES:
===============

✅ DO:
- Use specific metrics and achievements
- Create a sense of urgency or curiosity
- Include a clear link
- Add 1-2 relevant hashtags
- Keep it professional but engaging

❌ DON'T:
- Make it too long (keep under 3-4 lines)
- Use too many emojis (can look unprofessional)
- Be too salesy or pushy
- Forget to include the link
- Use all caps (looks like shouting)
